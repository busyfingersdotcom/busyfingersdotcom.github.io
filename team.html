Founder & CEO

Madhusatta Chaudhury

Brings over 25 years of experience working with international clients across diverse sectors, including Information Technology, Healthcare, Communications, and Vendor Recruitment. She holds a Master’s degree in Healthcare and an MBA in Human Resources.

Widely recognized for her extensive professional network and strong industry relationships, Madhusatta has built a reputation for connecting organizations with exceptional leadership talent. Her natural ability to engage with people, combined with outstanding communication and public relations skills, enables her to identify candidates who are not only qualified but also aligned with an organization’s culture and objectives.

Highly articulate and approachable, she works closely with both employers and candidates to understand their unique needs, ensuring successful and lasting placements. Her people-centric approach and deep understanding of talent dynamics have made her a trusted partner in leadership hiring across multiple industries.

Founder & CEO

Sanjeev Verma

With over four decades of entrepreneurial and business experience, Sanjeev's professional journey began at a young age when he started working part-time while still in school. These early experiences laid the foundation for a lifelong passion for business and entrepreneurship.

Over the years, he has worked across a diverse range of industries, including wool, communications, and export-import. He later founded and successfully managed his own IT company, specializing in medical transcription, software development, and branding services. Continuing his entrepreneurial journey, he has recently ventured into the perfumery industry, further demonstrating his ability to build and grow businesses across varied sectors.

Having spent decades as both an employer and an employee, Sanjeev possesses a unique understanding of the challenges, expectations, and aspirations on both sides of the hiring equation. This perspective enables him to bridge the gap between organizations and talent, with a focus on creating long-term, mutually beneficial partnerships rather than short-term placements.

His extensive business experience, practical leadership insights, and people-centric approach enable him to identify the right fit for every role while understanding the unique needs and challenges of both clients and candidates.

The Team Behind Every Search

While our founders provide strategic direction and client leadership, every successful search is supported by a dedicated team of researchers, recruiters, coordinators, and operations professionals working behind the scenes.

From talent mapping and market research to candidate engagement, interview coordination, and search execution, the team plays a critical role in ensuring that each assignment is managed with precision, responsiveness, and attention to detail.

Working closely with our leadership, they help maintain the high standards that define Busy Fingers, ensuring that clients receive a thoughtful, structured, and highly personalized hiring experience.

Together, we operate as one integrated team with a shared commitment: identifying, evaluating, and connecting organizations with the right leaders to drive long-term success.
